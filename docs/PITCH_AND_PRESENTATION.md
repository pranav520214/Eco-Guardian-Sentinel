# 🎤 Eco Guardian: Grand Finale Pitch & Stage Presentation Guide

This document outlines the official 14-slide pitch deck structure, stage speaker division, time budgeting, and defense strategies for the **Bharat Innovation Challenge 2.0 (BIC 2.0) Grand Finale** at Lovely Professional University (Nov 20–21, 2026).

---

## 1. Presentation Overview & Time Allocation

- **Total Pitch Window**: **5 Minutes (300 Seconds)** followed by **3 Minutes Jury Q&A**.
- **Stage Dynamics**:
  - **Naman Padhwal (Lead Pitch Presenter)**: Owns 80% of the spoken time, leading the opening narrative (Bhopal gas tragedy & modern smog crisis), market comparisons, municipal dispatch story, UN SDGs, business model, and jury close.
  - **Pranav Kumar Mishra (Founder & Hardware Lead)**: Delivers short, high-impact 1-to-2 sentence technical validations and commands the **Live Hardware Demonstration (Slide 6)**, physically demonstrating the working prototype.
  - **Faculty Mentor**: Keshav Aggarwal (STEM Cell, Army Public School Jalandhar Cantt).

### 12-Month Deliverables Committed:
1. **30 Live Operational Sentinels**: Deployed across 3 municipal wards and 5 school campuses with active telemetry feeds.
2. **DPDT-Compliant Data API Launch**: First paying enterprise data licensing contracts for micro-climate heatmaps.
3. **Proven Zomato-Style Civic Dispatch**: 500+ road defects logged and resolved under 48 hours in partnership with PWD.
4. **CPCB Calibration Audit Report**: Published 90-day accuracy and drift report co-located against CPCB reference stations.
5. **Incorporated Legal Entity**: Private Limited incorporation with parent/guardian director and provisional patents filed.
